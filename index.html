Power-Ups

Touch an item to collect it. Its icon moves to your top corner. Double-tap your side of the screen to activate it. Tap any item below for full details.

⚡Twin LightningTwo lightning strikes›

Collecting this gives you two separate charges. Each double-tap turns the ball into a lightning bolt for one second, doubles its speed, and sends it toward your opponent. Your position helps control whether the shot angles upward or downward.

🧊FreezeStops the opponent for 2 seconds›

Double-tap to freeze the opponent so they cannot move for two seconds. An active Goal Shield can reflect the freeze back at the player who used it.

🕵️StealTakes the other player’s stored item›

Takes the power-up currently stored in your opponent’s corner. When the opponent has no stored item, you receive a random power instead. A Goal Shield can block the theft and punish the attacker.

🛡️Goal ShieldCovers and protects the entire goal›

Creates a full-height barrier across your goal for about 6.5 seconds. It returns the ball toward the opponent and can reflect hostile powers such as Freeze.

🚀Speed MoveMakes your character move faster›

Boosts your movement speed for about seven seconds, helping you chase the ball, collect another item, or cover an open part of your goal.

💪Grow Big50% larger body and hit area›

Makes your complete character and collision area 50% larger. It lasts up to 30 seconds, but it ends immediately when either player scores a goal.

🪐Spiral ShotTwirls the ball toward the opponent›

Sends the ball forward in a looping spiral path for about 2.6 seconds. The twisting movement makes its exact path harder for the opponent to judge.

Only one power can be stored at a time. The icon in your top corner shows the power currently ready.
